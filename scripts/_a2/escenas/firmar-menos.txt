The waiting room of an airport in Mexico, in the middle of the day, with a wall of clean glass and rows of empty seats. They stand an arm length apart, neither of them touching the other.

She holds out a small paper cup of juice towards him with one hand, the head level and the mouth open in mid speech, the eyes on him.

He takes the cup with one hand and holds a small ice on a stick in the other, the head down towards the ice and the mouth closed, the tie loose at the open collar. Both faces are large in the upper half of the picture, apart from one another, and both pairs of eyes are open and clearly drawn, with a round dark iris and a white around it.

Behind them the glass wall shows a clean sky and a plain grey wing far away. Every wall and every surface is bare.
