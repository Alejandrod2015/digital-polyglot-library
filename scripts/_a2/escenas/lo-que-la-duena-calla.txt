The dining room of a boarding house in Peru at night, lit by one lamp above a long wooden table. She sits at the table with a plate of white rice in front of her, both hands in her lap, the head down and the mouth closed.

The other woman stands beside her chair holding a metal pot with both hands, tipping a little more rice onto the plate, the head turned away towards the far end of the room and the mouth open in mid speech, as if she were talking about nothing at all. Both faces are large in the upper half of the picture and both pairs of eyes are open and clearly drawn, with a round dark iris and a white around it.

On the table there is the plate, one glass of water and a plain cloth. Behind them a plain white wall.
