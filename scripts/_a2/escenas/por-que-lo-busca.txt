A small kitchen in Peru at night with no electric light, lit only by the small flame of an oil lamp standing on a shelf. She sits on a low wooden bench with both hands on her knees, the head lifted towards the other woman and the mouth open in mid speech.

Both faces are large in the upper half of the picture, lit warm from the side by the flame, and both pairs of eyes are open and clearly drawn, with a round dark iris and a white around it.

On the shelf there are the oil lamp and four plain glass jars with no paper on them. Behind them a plain dark wall.
