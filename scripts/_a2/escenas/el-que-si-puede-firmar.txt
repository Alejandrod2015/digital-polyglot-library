The bank of a wide brown river in Peru on a bright sunny morning, with no counter and no building. She stands on the mud at the edge of the water with a metal bucket at her feet, both hands loose at her sides, the head turned towards him and the mouth closed.

He stands a few steps up the bank, turned back towards her with the body already half away, one hand lifted and the mouth open in mid speech, as if he had changed his mind on the spot. Both faces are large in the upper half of the picture, one on each side of the middle, and both pairs of eyes are open and clearly drawn, with a round dark iris and a white around it.

Behind them a long flat raft of tied logs floating at the edge, and a green wall of trees on the far side under a clean blue sky.
