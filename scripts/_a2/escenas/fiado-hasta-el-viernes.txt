A covered market aisle in Mexico early in the day, quiet and empty of anybody else. They stand at the two ends of the same fruit table, an arm length apart, both of them behind it.

She stands at the near end with two oranges still in one hand and the other hand open and lifted, the head turned to look away down the aisle, the mouth open in mid speech.

He stands at the far end of the same table with both hands flat on the same wood, turned towards her, the head level and the mouth closed in a small knowing smile. Both faces are large in the upper half of the picture, apart from one another, and both pairs of eyes are open and clearly drawn, with a round dark iris and a white around it.

On the table between them there are oranges in a wooden box and a stack of empty boxes. Behind them the aisle is empty and every other stall is a plain wooden frame, bare wood only, with nothing hanging above it. Every wall and every surface is bare.
