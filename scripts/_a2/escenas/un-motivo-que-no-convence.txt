A river port in Peru at dawn under a grey sky, seen from the bank. She stands behind a plain wooden counter set up in the open air, both hands flat on the wood, the head level and turned towards him, the mouth closed.

He stands on the other side of the counter an arm length away, turned to face her, one hand open at his side and the mouth open in mid speech. Both faces are large in the upper half of the picture, one on each side of the middle, and both pairs of eyes are open and clearly drawn, with a round dark iris and a white around it.

Behind them a wide brown river with a long wooden canoe tied to a post at the bank, and a green wall of trees on the far side. The counter is bare.
