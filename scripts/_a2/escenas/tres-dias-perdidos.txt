A wide brown river in Peru in the morning. The water fills the whole lower half of the picture and both people are standing above it.

He stands on a long flat raft of tied logs out on the water, one hand on a rope and a wet sack at his feet, the head turned back towards the bank and the mouth open in mid speech.

She stands at the top of the bank on the right, above the water and clear of it, one hand lifted at the height of the shoulder, the head turned towards him and the mouth closed. Both faces are large in the upper half of the picture, one on each side of the middle, and both pairs of eyes are open and clearly drawn, with a round dark iris and a white around it.

Behind everything a green wall of trees.
