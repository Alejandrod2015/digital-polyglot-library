A covered market aisle in Mexico at half past five in the morning, still half dark, with the other stalls along the aisle shuttered and dark behind them. Her stall alone is open and lit by one bulb.

She stands behind her fruit table stacking wooden boxes of mango, both hands on a box, the head turned towards him and the mouth closed.

He stands in the aisle an arm length away, in front of his own shuttered stall, one hand pointing back down the dark aisle and the mouth open in mid speech. Both faces are large in the upper half of the picture, apart from one another, and both pairs of eyes are open and clearly drawn, with a round dark iris and a white around it.

On her table there are ripe mangoes in wooden boxes. The floor of the aisle is wet stone. Every wall and every surface is bare.
