A covered market aisle in Mexico at the end of the day, in shade, quiet and empty of anybody else. Her fruit table is bare and stripped, with the metal shutter of the stall half down behind it.

She sits on a low wooden bench at one end of the bare table, both hands on her knees, the head lifted towards him and the mouth closed.

He stands at the same table an arm length away, one hand just leaving a small folded stack of banknotes on the wood, seen edge on so only the folded edges show, the head level and the mouth open in mid speech. Both faces are large in the upper half of the picture, apart from one another, and both pairs of eyes are open and clearly drawn, with a round dark iris and a white around it.

On the table there is only the folded money. Behind them the aisle is empty and every other stall is a plain wooden frame, bare wood only, with nothing hanging above it. Every wall and every surface is bare.
