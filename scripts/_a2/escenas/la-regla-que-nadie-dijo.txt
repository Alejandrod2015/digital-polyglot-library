A small bedroom in a rented house in Salento, Colombia, in the middle of the day. She stands at the foot of a narrow bed, holding both corners of a folded bedspread she has just laid out, hands still on the cloth, looking across at the other woman.

The other woman stands in the doorway with a kettle in one hand, her other hand at her side and well away from the bed, chin down, watching the bed instead of her face, saying nothing.

In the room there is the narrow bed with the plain cream bedspread, a bare wooden side table, a wooden wardrobe with its door shut, and a window with wooden shutters open to green hills. There are no tools, no boxes and no packaging anywhere in the room.
