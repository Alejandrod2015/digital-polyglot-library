An old kitchen in a house in Salento, Colombia, in the morning. She stands at a bare wooden table pouring water slowly from a kettle into a cloth coffee filter held over a jug, both hands busy, watching the water.

The other woman stands close beside her holding the wooden ring of the cloth filter steady with one hand, head turned to watch what she is doing, mouth closed.

On the table there is the jug under the cloth filter, two plain white cups, and a single drop of coffee on the wood. Behind them a whitewashed wall and a window with wooden shutters open to green hills. The table holds nothing else.
