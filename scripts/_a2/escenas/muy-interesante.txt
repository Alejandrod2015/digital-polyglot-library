An office corridor in Mexico, in the middle of the day, with plain painted walls and a grey floor. He stands facing her with the shoulders open and the mouth in a small pleased smile, one hand still on the handle of a closed door behind him.

She stands an arm length away turned towards him, both hands at her sides, the head level, the mouth open in mid speech and the face without any smile at all. Both faces are large in the upper half of the picture, one on each side of the middle, and both pairs of eyes are open and clearly drawn, with a round dark iris and a white around it.

Behind them the corridor runs away to a window of clean daylight. The walls are bare.
