The open courtyard of a boarding house in Peru in the afternoon, with white stone walls and a wet stone floor that shines. She stands at the foot of a stone stair with one hand on the rail, the head turned back towards the other woman and the mouth closed.

The other woman stands a step away on the wet stone, both hands together in front of her, the head level and the mouth open in mid speech. Both faces are large in the upper half of the picture, one on each side of the middle, and both pairs of eyes are open and clearly drawn, with a round dark iris and a white around it.

Behind them a plain white wall and one green plant in a clay pot. There is nothing else in the courtyard.
