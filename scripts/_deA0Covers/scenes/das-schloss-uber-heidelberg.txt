Inside the cool stone cellar under the ruined red sandstone castle above Heidelberg. An enormous plain wooden barrel as big as a whole room fills the back of the cellar, its boards completely bare and unmarked. Sophie stands in front of it with one hand raised, explaining; Hannah looks up at the sheer size of it; Elias is whistling, his lips pursed, head tipped back. Thick pale stone walls, a shaft of daylight from a small high opening.
