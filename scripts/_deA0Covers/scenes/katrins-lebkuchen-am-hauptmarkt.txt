A busy Friday morning at a gingerbread stall on the big market square in Nuernberg, the ornate church front behind. The stall counter is stacked with round gingerbread biscuits and colourful old painted tin boxes decorated only with simple flower patterns and no writing at all; there are no price signs, no boards and no labels anywhere on the stall. Katrin leans over the counter holding out one piece of gingerbread to Hannah, who takes it; one customer waits to one side. Warm morning light, saturated reds and golds.

IN FRAME (3 people, all adults): Hannah, 32, straight dark brown hair to the shoulders, mustard yellow jacket, white t-shirt, blue jeans, small dark green backpack; Katrin, 48, dark brown hair with no grey pinned in a loose bun, plum purple apron, cream blouse, long dark skirt; a customer, a woman of 40 with shoulder length blonde hair in a blue coat, waiting patiently with a basket on her arm.

Faces in three quarter view so both eyes read clearly, calm and absorbed in the moment, turned towards the scene and not towards the viewer.
