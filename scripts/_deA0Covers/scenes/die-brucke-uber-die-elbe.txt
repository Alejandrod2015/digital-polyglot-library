Hannah stands at the middle of the bridge with one hand flat on the parapet and her backpack on the stone beside her; Elias stands close, a paper cup of coffee in one hand, the other arm stretched out towards the misted towers on the far bank. A long low riverboat passes beneath them. Cool silver light.
