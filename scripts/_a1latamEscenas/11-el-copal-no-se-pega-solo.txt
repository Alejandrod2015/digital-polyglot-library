The same room in Oaxaca, Mexico, at night. One warm bulb overhead, the rest of
the room in soft shadow, whitewashed walls, the low wooden table in the middle.
On the table: the painted wooden alebrije lying on its side, its detached carved
leg, a roll of plain beige sticky tape and a small plain unmarked glue bottle
with no label at all. Exactly two people, both adults, at mid distance.

MATEO sits at the table on the left, leaning close over the work, seen
three-quarters. Age 30, Mexican, TALL and LEAN, narrow face, short black hair,
clean-shaven with NO beard and NO stubble, skin medium warm brown, plain GREY
t-shirt, dark blue jeans. He presses the carved leg against the body of the
figure with both thumbs and holds his breath, mouth closed, eyebrows drawn
together, concentrating hard.

LA TÍA ROSA stands in the doorway on the right, leaning on the frame, NOT
coming in. Age 50, Mexican, SHORT and BROAD, a head shorter than Mateo, wide
face, round full cheeks, heavy eyebrows, skin deep warm brown, hair BLACK,
straight, pulled back flat and tied at the nape, NO grey, NO white. She wears a
plain DEEP PURPLE blouse and a long dark skirt, arms folded. She watches him
work with a small closed-mouth smile, patient, amused.

Nobody else is in the picture. NO other figures, not even blurred, NO children,
NO teenagers, NO elderly people, NO grey or white hair.
