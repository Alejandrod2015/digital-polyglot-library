A small front room of a house in Getsemaní, Cartagena, Colombia, at six in the
morning. Pale early light through a tall shuttered window, walls washed in warm
ochre, a wooden table, a plastic chair, a doorway open onto a bright colonial
street with bougainvillea outside. Exactly two women, at mid distance, waist-up.

THE TWO WOMEN MUST NOT SHARE A FACE. They are built as opposites and a viewer
must tell them apart by the head alone, with the clothes covered up.

LUCÍA sits at the table on the left, three-quarter view. Age 30. TALL and SLIM,
long neck, narrow oval face, high cheekbones, thin arched eyebrows. Skin LIGHT
warm brown. Hair BLACK and TIGHTLY CURLY, shoulder-length, loose and full around
her face. She wears a plain RUST-ORANGE sleeveless blouse and dark jeans. A
small dark mobile phone lies FLAT on the table in front of her, face down, and
she looks at it without touching it, mouth closed, eyebrows raised, listening.

LA MADRE stands in the doorway on the right, turned toward Lucía. Age 52. SHORT
and BROAD, a full head shorter than Lucía, wide square face, round full cheeks,
heavy straight eyebrows, thick neck, sturdy shoulders. Skin DEEP DARK BROWN,
several shades darker than Lucía. Hair BLACK, DEAD STRAIGHT, pulled back TIGHT
and flat against her head, NOT curly, NOT loose, NO grey, NO white. She wears a
plain OLIVE-GREEN house dress with short sleeves and holds a dish cloth in one
hand. She has stopped mid-step and looks at the phone, mouth closed, still.

Nobody else is in the picture and the street through the doorway is EMPTY. NO
other figures, not even blurred, NO neighbours, NO crowd, NO silhouettes, NO
children, NO teenagers, NO elderly people, NO grey or white hair.

NOTHING in the scene can carry writing or numbers: the phone is FACE DOWN with a
blank back, NO screen visible, NO photographs, NO papers, NO calendar, NO
posters, NO labels, NO lettering on clothing. Absolutely NO text, NO letters, NO
numbers, NO words anywhere in the image.

Both faces have real expressive eyes with visible irises and eyebrows, smooth
